Remote Patient Monitoring in Hospital Setting With proRITHM IR Platform & Devices

AI-enabled remote patient monitoring improves chronic disease management in hospital settings.

Download Case StudyTalk to Our Team

Overview

The proRITHM platform enables healthcare facilities to track hospitalized patients remotely through integrated devices and AI-driven analysis.

End Users

Patient

Individual receiving remote monitoring during hospitalization.

Nurse

Clinical staff responsible for patient oversight and intervention.

Physician

Attending doctor managing treatment decisions.

proRITHM Device

Medical equipment collecting and transmitting health metrics.

Hospital IT Support

Technical team ensuring system functionality.

proRITHM IR Platform

Central monitoring dashboard for healthcare providers.

Key Preconditions

“The hospital has implemented a remote patient monitoring system utilising the proRITHM IR Platform and device.” Additionally, staff training, patient consent, and candidate identification are necessary prerequisites.

Standard Process Flow

  1. 1

    Patient Admission

    Eligible patients receive devices and setup assistance upon arrival.

  2. 2

    Data Transmission

    The proRITHM Device securely transmits vital information to the integrated hospital EHR system.

  3. 3

    Nurse Monitoring

    Staff receive alerts, assess situations, and document interventions in patient records.

  4. 4

    Patient Education

    Clinical teams educate patients and encourage active participation in their care management.

  5. 5

    Continuous Monitoring

    The system collects heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, temperature, and activity data throughout the hospital stay.

  6. 6

    Real-Time Analysis

    The platform generates alerts when abnormal trends or critical indicators are detected.

  7. 7

    Physician Access

    Doctors review data remotely and collaborate with nursing staff on treatment adjustments.

Exceptions

Technical disruptions, medical emergencies requiring immediate bedside care, and patient opt-out requests are addressed as needed.
