FAQs About proRITHM

The proRITHM IR platform operates by integrating a range of devices and technologies to collect and analyze patient data in real-time. This data includes vital signs such as heart rate, blood pressure, temperature, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, and heart rate variability. The platform utilizes advanced algorithms and artificial intelligence to interpret this data, identifying patterns, trends, and abnormalities that may indicate changes in a patient’s health status. The proRITHM IR device is a key component of this platform, specifically designed for remote patient monitoring. It is equipped with sensors to measure various physiological parameters and securely transmits this data to the proRITHM IR platform for analysis.
